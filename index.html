Desmos → Relative G-Code

Paste a Desmos share link. This pulls the image and any coordinate points you placed in the graph, traces the figure's outline, and builds relative (G91) G-code — ready to paste into ncviewer.com.
1. Load your Desmos graph
This page's local server fetches the graph for you — "Fetch Graph" should just work off the link. This box is only a backup for the rare case Desmos itself is unreachable. If needed: open your Desmos link, open the browser console (F12), and run:
fetch(location.href,{headers:{Accept:'application/json'}}).then(r=>r.text()).then(t=>copy(t))
That copies the graph JSON to your clipboard — paste it into the box above.
